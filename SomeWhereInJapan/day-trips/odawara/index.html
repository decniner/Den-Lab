FIELD NOTE 05 · KANAGAWA

Odawara at the castle gate

Odawara’s castle is an easy walk from the station, but the satisfying visit is the approach through the old gates and moat before climbing to the keep. Add the Samurai Museum, then browse the town’s food shops.

From Tokyo Station by JR Tokaido Line

Best for Castle history, local food and an easy rail day

Time 5.5–9 hours

Effort Easy town walk; tower stairs

Enter through the historic approach

  1. Take JR to Odawara Station. The standard Tokaido Line is the budget estimate. Shinkansen is faster but costs more; check current fares and service before departure.
  2. Walk toward Ohoribata-dori. The castle is around ten minutes from the station. Follow the moat and enter through the Umadashi Gate area, then continue toward the main keep.
  3. Visit the castle keep and Samurai Museum. The keep admission currently includes the Samurai Museum; the Ninja Museum is separately priced and can be added when open.
  4. Finish back in town. Browse local shops and market-style food stops near the station. In good weather, leave time to explore the castle park rather than rushing directly back.

Planning estimate

About ¥5,500–¥6,500 for a half-day visit, including an estimated ¥2,980 return rail fare, ¥1,000 castle admission and ¥1,500–¥2,500 meal allowance. The city fare page may lag fare revisions: verify current rail pricing before departure. Shinkansen, local buses, extra museums and shopping are not included.

Season, effort and access

Spring blossom season is popular; summer can be hot with limited shade. Castle tower stairs are steep and step-free access to the keep is limited. In wet weather, keep the outdoor moat walk short and spend more time in the keep and museum. The official castle advises public transport because weekend road traffic and parking can be difficult.

Exact points to pin

  • Odawara Station — main rail arrival
  • Ohoribata-dori and Umadashi Gate — castle approach
  • Odawara Castle keep — 6-1 Jonai, Odawara
  • Samurai Museum — within Odawara Castle grounds
  • Odawara Station shopping streets — food stop and return
