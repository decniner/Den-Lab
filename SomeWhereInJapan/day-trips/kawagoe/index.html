FIELD NOTE 01 · SAITAMA

Kawagoe by old streets

The former castle town is close enough for an unhurried day. Its storehouse facades, narrow lanes and bell tower reward walking slowly, with snack breaks between landmarks.

From Tokyo Station via Ikebukuro

Best for History, sweets, easy city walking

Time 4.5–8 hours door to door

Effort Easy; several kilometres on foot

A good fit when

A castle town that kept its old street plan Easy, mostly level city walking; expect several kilometres on mixed pavement.

Consider another day if

The old streets can feel busy on weekends and holidays; arrive early for a calmer first hour.

Take the day in layers

  1. Reach Kawagoe. Tokyo Metro to Ikebukuro, then Tobu Tojo Line to Kawagoe; allow roughly 45–60 minutes each way including transfer and station connections. Check the operator fare before travel.
  2. Start at the castle grounds. From Kawagoe Station, walk or use a local bus to Kawagoe Castle Honmaru Goten. Its historic rooms offer context for the town’s merchant streets. Confirm weekday closure before setting out.
  3. Follow the old town toward Toki no Kane. Explore the Kurazukuri merchant street and bell tower, then pause at Kashiya Yokocho for traditional sweets. The route is flexible: shorten it by turning back from the bell tower.
  4. Leave room for lunch. Choose a local restaurant around the old town; menus and prices vary and are not included beyond the meal allowance below.

Planning estimate

About ¥2,600–¥4,000 per person, including a round-trip Tokyo–Ikebukuro and Ikebukuro–Kawagoe rail estimate, planned admission where listed, and ¥1,500–¥2,500 for a meal. Local buses, snacks and shopping can add cost. Approximate transit time is 1.5–2.1 hours round trip; day duration depends on pace.

Season and access

The historic center is enjoyable across much of the year. Spring and autumn are generally more comfortable for walking; weekends and holidays can be crowded, so arrive early. Pavements are mostly level but sidewalks and older streets can be busy or uneven. For rain, focus on the covered museum or indoor castle visit and shorten the street walk.
