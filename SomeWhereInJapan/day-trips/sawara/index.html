FIELD NOTE 02 · CHIBA

Sawara along the canal

Merchant houses and bridges line the Ono River in this small historic center. It is a slower day with an important constraint: plan around the limited direct bus timetable.

From Tokyo Station Yaesu South Exit

Best for Canal-side history and museums

Time 8–10.5 hours

Effort Easy town walking

Build around the bus

  1. Book the highway bus. Kanto Railway operates Tokyo–Sawara services. Departures are limited; choose the outbound and return pair first, confirm the current stop and timetable, and buy a seat if required.
  2. Walk the Ono River. From the Sawara stop or JR Sawara Station, head to the historic district and Chukei Bridge. The riverfront and merchant buildings form the heart of the visit.
  3. Choose a museum pairing. Suigo Sawara Float Museum documents the festival floats. Inoh Tadataka Museum and his former residence add the story of the surveyor. Confirm the combo ticket and closing day before travel.
  4. Return early to the stop. Allow a generous buffer; do not assume another direct bus will follow soon.

Planning estimate

About ¥6,100–¥7,100 per person: approximately ¥3,800 return highway bus, ¥800 planned combined museum admission and ¥1,500–¥2,500 meal allowance. Optional purchases and local taxis are extra. The coach journey is about 1 hour 20 minutes each way in the operator timetable, before traffic.

Season and access

The historic streets are walkable in most seasons. Festival periods in July and October are especially lively and can bring crowding or traffic arrangements. Summer heat and rain can make the open river walk uncomfortable. Museum visits provide an indoor alternative. Historic paving can be uneven; confirm step-free access directly with each facility.

Exact points to pin

  • Tokyo Station Yaesu South Exit — outbound bus boarding
  • Sawara (Suigo Sawara) highway-bus stop — confirm exact current stop label
  • Ono River and Chukei Bridge
  • Suigo Sawara Float Museum — 1722-1 Sawarai, Katori
  • Inoh Tadataka Museum — 1722-1 Sawarai, Katori
