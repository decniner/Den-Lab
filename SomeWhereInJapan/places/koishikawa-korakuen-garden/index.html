PLACE-SPECIFIC PLANNING · Kanto

Plan your visit to Koishikawa Korakuen Garden

A walk through a designed landscape that keeps changing its frame: pond, hill, bridge, then a quiet view that hides the city for a moment.

Mapped order · 4 stops

  1. 01 · East Gate

    Start beside Tokyo Dome City, then step from the modern city into the garden’s carefully framed landscape.

  2. 02 · Daisensui Pond

    The garden’s central water scene changes as you circle it; look for the borrowed landscape of distant hills.

  3. 03 · Engetsu Bridge

    A compact stone bridge creates a moon-like reflection when the water and light align.

  4. 04 · West Gate

    Finish near Iidabashi for a convenient rail connection; confirm which gate is open on your visit.
