A PLACE-LED FIELD GUIDE

Yakushima, ancient green

Approximate area reference · attraction

Moss, cedar giants, and rain-silvered trails on an island all its own.

See the place map ↓Plan your arrival

Yakushima's forest feels ancient because the island makes time visible: rain-darkened cedar roots, moss-soft stones and trees whose trunks hold centuries of weather. Shiratani Unsuikyo is a compelling first walk, with marked trails through cedar and moss forest and optional longer loops for prepared hikers. It is not a theme-park path; rain, slippery roots and steep sections change the effort quickly. The island also has waterfalls, coastal settlements and the Yakusugi Museum, so a thoughtful visit balances mountain forest with a lower-elevation stop. The famous Jomon Sugi hike is a separate, strenuous full-day undertaking and should not be added casually to this short route. Spring and autumn are often comfortable, but rain is possible in every season. Check trail and bus status, bring layers and carry out everything you bring in. Shiratani Unsuikyo trailhead. GETTING THERE

Choose the right arrival

Flights connect Yakushima Airport with Kagoshima, while ferries and high-speed boats serve Miyanoura and Anbo ports. Local buses and rental cars connect villages; Shiratani Unsuikyo trailhead is reached by bus from Miyanoura when services operate. Kagoshima Airport and Kagoshima Chuo Station are the main gateways. Bus frequencies are limited, so check return times or arrange a car before hiking.

Choose a marked Shiratani Unsuikyo loop suited to your fitness, then visit the Yakusugi Museum or a nearby coast viewpoint on a separate transport leg. Allow 4-7 hours for the forest walk. Jomon Sugi is a strenuous full-day hike requiring an early start and separate preparation.

SEASON & CONDITIONSYakushima is rainy year-round. Spring and autumn are comfortable for hiking, while winter brings cold mountain conditions and summer brings heat and heavy rain. Trail access can close because of weather; check the town and forestry notices on the day.

WHERE TO STAY

Stay in Miyanoura for port access or Anbo for southern routes and the Yakusugi Museum; reserve a night before and after long hikes to protect your schedule.
