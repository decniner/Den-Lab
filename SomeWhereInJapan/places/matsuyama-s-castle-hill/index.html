A PLACE-LED FIELD GUIDE

Matsuyama’s castle hill

Approximate area reference · attraction

Ride up to a hilltop castle, then unwind in Dogo, a hot-spring neighborhood steeped in literary history.

See the place map ↓Plan your arrival

Matsuyama Castle still crowns the city's central hill, and the climb reveals why this is more than a photogenic skyline. The surviving keep and connected towers show how a defensive complex could occupy a compact mountaintop, while the view opens across Matsuyama toward the sea. Ride the ropeway or chairlift for an easier ascent, then explore the castle grounds at walking pace. Below, Ninomaru Historical Garden traces the former castle residence and offers a quieter way back into town. From there, the streetcar connects to Dogo Onsen, making a satisfying second chapter rather than an unrelated add-on. Spring blossoms and autumn foliage frame the hill; summer heat is tiring on the slopes. Castle access may involve steep stairs, so check accessibility and wear stable shoes. Okaido tram stop. Use the city tram for the central approach; walk through the castle town toward the ropeway street. Matsuyama Castle ropeway. Choose ropeway or chairlift for the ascent; follow current operation notices and do not drive to the hilltop. Matsuyama Castle keep. Explore the surviving tower complex and panoramic views; interior stairs are steep and may be challenging. Ninomaru Historical Garden. Descend to the former residence garden for a quieter finish before continuing by tram toward Dogo Onsen.

GETTING THERE

Choose the right arrival

Matsuyama Station connects by JR to other parts of Shikoku; Matsuyama Airport has buses to the city center. From JR Matsuyama Station, take the streetcar to Okaido and walk to the ropeway street. Drivers can use the castle parking area, but cars cannot reach the mountaintop. Dogo Onsen is linked by tram, not a direct walk from the castle.

Take the ropeway or chairlift to the castle, explore the keep and grounds, descend toward Ninomaru Historical Garden and finish with a streetcar ride to Dogo Onsen if time allows. Allow 4-6 hours for castle and garden, longer with Dogo. Confirm last ropeway times.

WHERE TO STAY

Stay near Okaido for easy castle access, or in Dogo Onsen for a bathhouse-centered evening with a short streetcar connection to the city center.
