THE FLOWER COUNTRY · HOKKAIDO

Furano
in bloom.

A summer day from Furano Station

Follow the colour north through Nakafurano, pause for the taste of local milk, then let the day soften beneath the trees at Ningle Terrace.

Follow the mapped day ↓Plan your arrival

Furano is not only a purple hillside. It is a whole summer day stitched together from open fields, Hokkaido dairy country and a small forest of craft cottages. Come for the flowers, but give the region enough time to change its mood: bright and spacious in the afternoon, green and quiet among the pines, then warmly lit as evening arrives.

TIME YOUR VISIT

A short season, with its own rhythm

Lavender varieties do not bloom on the same schedule. The exact colour shifts with weather and cultivar, so treat these as a guide and check the current flower report before travelling.

Late June · first colour

Catch the early varieties

The first lavender begins to open. The fields feel less like a single peak and more like summer beginning to arrive.

Mid-July · classic Furano

Plan for the signature view

The Furano Tourism Association says the widely planted Okamurasaki variety usually reaches its peak around mid-July. This is the strongest window for the famous purple slopes, though bloom dates vary by year.

Late July–early August · later bloom

Look beyond one field

Later varieties can carry colour into early August. Some fields are cut back during August, so check the latest local update rather than relying on a fixed calendar.

Outside lavender season? Farm Tomita’s flower gardens change through the year, and its greenhouse may show lavender even when the outdoor fields are not in bloom. Check the farm’s current information before making a special trip.

THREE DISTINCT MOMENTS

What makes this day feel like Furano

01 · THE FIELDS

Farm Tomita

Walk between the flower bands instead of viewing the hillside from one point. Look for the changing stripes, the mountain horizon, and the way the colour depends on where you stand.

02 · THE TASTE

Furano Cheese Factory

Look through the windows into the production rooms, then try the local dairy story in edible form. The factory is known for cheeses made with Furano milk, including wine-infused cheese, alongside ice milk and pizza.

03 · THE EVENING

Ningle Terrace

Small timber cottages sit beneath the New Furano Prince Hotel among trees and a stream. Browse the makers’ work slowly; toward evening, the forest path and its lights give the day a completely different finish.

THE DAY, IN ORDER · DRIVING LOOP

Fields first. Forest at the end.

Start at Furano Station and head north to Farm Tomita while the daylight is bright. Return toward town for the Cheese Factory, then finish at Ningle Terrace, where the timber shops and wooded paths make more sense as the afternoon fades. Allow most of a day; opening hours, flower stops and time spent browsing all change the pace.

GETTING THERE

Choose your way into the valley

By train from Sapporo: Regular rail routes typically connect through Takikawa. In summer, JR Hokkaido may run the seasonal Furano Lavender Express directly from Sapporo; it is date-specific and requires the appropriate limited-express ticket. Check the live JR timetable before building a same-day plan.

By train from Asahikawa: Take the JR Furano Line to Furano Station. The Furano–Biei Norokko sightseeing train and Lavender Field Station are seasonal services; confirm the operating calendar before counting on them.

By air: Asahikawa Airport is the practical gateway for this itinerary. Furano Bus’s Lavender-go connects the airport with Furano, but departures are limited and may not match every flight. There is no JR station at the airport. A rental car is another option; check current road conditions, especially in winter. From New Chitose Airport, compare JR connections with the reserved Furano-go highway bus before choosing your arrival plan.

By bus or car between stops: Furano’s Lavender Bus serves key sightseeing areas, including the New Furano Prince Hotel/Ningle Terrace area, but service varies by season. The Cheese Factory is about a nine-minute drive from Furano Station; the tourism association lists Ningle Terrace about 15 minutes by car. For the complete three-stop day, a rental car or pre-booked taxi is the simplest fit.
