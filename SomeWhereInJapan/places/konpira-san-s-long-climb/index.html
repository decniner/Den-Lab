Konpira-san’s long climb

A pilgrimage stairway with a meaningful choice built into it: the main shrine is already a substantial climb; the inner shrine adds a demanding extension.

The approach begins among shops and gives way to stone steps, cedar shade and small shrines. The main Kotohira-gu shrine is reached after 785 steps. The inner shrine adds 583 more; treat it as a separate effort, not an automatic add-on, and check its access hours.
