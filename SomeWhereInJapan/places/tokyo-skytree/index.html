Tokyo Skytree

attraction · JAPAN

Ride above the city from the world’s tallest freestanding broadcasting tower, then explore the riverside neighborhood and Solamachi below. Book a timed observatory ticket for sunset or clear-weather views toward Mount Fuji.

Culture

TOKYO SKYTREE is more than a skyline stop: the approach is part of the visit. Its official transport guide points visitors to either Tokyo Skytree Station or Oshiage (Skytree-mae) Station, connecting the observation visit to the surrounding Sumida district.

GETTING THERE

Choose how to arrive

The official venue identifies Tokyo Skytree Station on the Tobu Skytree Line and Oshiage Station on the Hanzomon Line as convenient arrivals. The official site lists the address as 1-1-2 Oshiage, Sumida-ku, Tokyo.

  • Rail: Arrive at Tokyo Skytree Station or Oshiage (Skytree-mae) Station; use the official access page for specific approaches. (Access by public transportation)
