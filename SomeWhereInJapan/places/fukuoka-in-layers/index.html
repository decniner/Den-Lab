More details

Begin in the compact temple streets near Hakata, cross the city for a slower afternoon around the castle moat, then return for a yatai dinner if the weather and stalls cooperate. This route is a planning outline, not a timed tour. Check live directions and each venue’s current access information.

When to go

Spring and autumn are comfortable for walking. Winters are mild, while summers are hot, humid and often rainy. Keep the yatai dinner flexible because individual stalls may close for weather or a day off. Visit Fukuoka · climate and travel tips ↗

Route and community context: This stop order follows the existing city route record and its cited visitor sources. It is a curated route, not a representative community poll or a definitive ranking of the city’s “best” places. Some stop times or venue details may change.

Stop-by-stop guide and directions

  1. 01

    Kushida Shrine

    Kushida Shrine, Kamikawabata, Hakata, Fukuoka, Japan

    A living shrine at the heart of Hakata, closely tied to the city’s festival life. Visit quietly and check whether festival preparations affect access.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  2. 02

    Hakata Old Town · Tochoji Temple

    Hakata Old Town · Tochoji Temple, Gion, Hakata, Fukuoka, Japan

    A short walk links temples and historic streets that recall Hakata’s long trading history. Confirm individual temple rules and visiting hours before entering.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  3. 03

    Fukuoka Castle Ruins & Maizuru Park

    Fukuoka Castle Ruins & Maizuru Park, Jonai, Chuo, Fukuoka, Japan

    Stone walls and the old moat spread across a broad city park. The remains are easy to explore at your own pace, with open views across the green space.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  4. 04

    Ohori Park

    Ohori Park, Ohorikoen, Chuo, Fukuoka, Japan

    The loop around the water is a calm counterpoint to Hakata’s busy streets. Take the path slowly and leave time for the Japanese garden if it is open.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  5. 05

    Tenjin yatai area

    Tenjin yatai area, Tenjin, Chuo, Fukuoka, Japan

    If stalls are operating, finish with a meal at a yatai. Each stall is independent and may close for weather or a day off; bring a flexible dinner plan.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
