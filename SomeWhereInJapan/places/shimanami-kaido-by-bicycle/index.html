A PLACE-LED FIELD GUIDE

Shimanami Kaido by bicycle

Approximate area reference · walking-route

Cross a chain of islands and elegant bridges at cycling pace, with citrus groves and sea views en route.

See the place map ↓Plan your arrival

The Shimanami Kaido turns island-hopping into a journey measured by bridges, citrus groves and sea crossings. The full signed cycling route links Onomichi and Imabari across about 70 kilometers, but beginners should not mistake that for an easy casual ride: bridge ramps climb, supplies are sparse on bridges, and weather matters. A more welcoming plan is to ride one section, stop on an island and stay overnight, then decide whether to continue. Mukaishima, Innoshima and Ikuchijima each have a different rhythm, from port lanes to bridge viewpoints and citrus orchards. Rent the right bicycle, check drop-off rules and plan the return before setting out. Spring and autumn are ideal; summer heat demands extra water. Follow the blue route markings, use ferries where advised, and leave room for spontaneous seaside breaks. Onomichi cycle terminal. Collect a suitable bike, helmet and route map; confirm drop-off rules before leaving the terminal. Mukaishima ferry. Use the short passenger ferry crossing with your bicycle where the official route recommends it; follow crew instructions. Innoshima Bridge viewpoint. Approach the bridge using signed cycle ramps and take care on shared paths and descents. Setoda and Ikuchijima. Use the island town as a meal or overnight stop; check bike return and onward ferry or bus options.

GETTING THERE

Choose the right arrival

Start from JR Onomichi Station or Sunrise Itoyama near Imabari; both are reached by rail and regional buses. Hiroshima and Matsuyama airports serve opposite ends of the route. The Onomichi-Mukaishima crossing is by ferry for cyclists; cars use the expressway and cannot follow every cycling access ramp. Rental terminals and one-way bike returns vary, so reserve and verify your endpoint transport.

For a first outing, ride a shorter signed section from Onomichi via ferry to Mukaishima and onward toward Innoshima, then return using a confirmed ferry or overnight stay. The full 70 km usually takes 7-9 hours with breaks and suits prepared riders. Plan water and return transport first.

WHERE TO STAY

Stay in Onomichi for the Hiroshima-side start, Imabari near Sunrise Itoyama for an Ehime-side start, or book an island guesthouse to split the route.
