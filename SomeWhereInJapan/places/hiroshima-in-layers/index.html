More details

A thoughtful route through the Peace Memorial Park before shifting to the castle grounds and the lively central arcade. Leave time for the museum and approach the memorial sites with care. This route is a planning outline, not a timed tour. Check live directions and each venue’s current access information.

When to go

Spring and autumn are usually more comfortable for the outdoor sections. In summer, allow shade and water breaks. Check current museum and castle hours before visiting, and approach memorial spaces quietly. Dive! Hiroshima · official visitor guide ↗

Route and community context: This stop order follows the existing city route record and its cited visitor sources. It is a curated route, not a representative community poll or a definitive ranking of the city’s “best” places. Some stop times or venue details may change.

Stop-by-stop guide and directions

  1. 01

    Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park

    Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park, Nakajima-cho, Naka, Hiroshima, Japan

    Begin outdoors with the river, memorials and landscape of the park. Keep voices low and allow visitors who are there to remember space and privacy.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  2. 02

    Atomic Bomb Dome

    Atomic Bomb Dome, Otemachi, Naka, Hiroshima, Japan

    View the preserved structure from outside the surrounding barriers. The site is a place of remembrance; follow posted access restrictions and avoid treating it as a casual backdrop.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  3. 03

    Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum

    Hiroshima Peace Memorial Museum, Nakajima-cho, Naka, Hiroshima, Japan

    The museum’s exhibits are emotionally demanding. Take the visit at your own pace and check the current ticketing and opening information before arrival.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  4. 04

    Hiroshima Castle & grounds

    Hiroshima Castle & grounds, Motomachi, Naka, Hiroshima, Japan

    The reconstructed keep and moat offer a different chapter of the city’s history. Confirm current museum hours and any access changes before making this the final stop.

    Open this stop in Google Maps ↗
  5. 05

    Hondori Shopping Arcade

    Hondori Shopping Arcade, Naka, Hiroshima, Japan

    End in the covered centre with a meal and a return to the pace of everyday Hiroshima. It is an easy point from which to catch a streetcar back toward the station.
