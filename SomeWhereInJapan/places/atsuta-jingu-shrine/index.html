Atsuta Jingu Shrine

attraction · JAPAN

One of Nagoya’s most important Shinto shrines, set in a deep green precinct of old trees and quiet paths. Visit respectfully and check access notices before attending ceremonies or special events.

Nagoya: castle, street food & shrine forest

  1. 01

    Naka, Nagoya

    Nagoya Castle & Hommaru Palace

    Nagoya Castle & Hommaru Palace, Naka, Nagoya, Japan

    Give the palace interiors and castle grounds their own time. The main keep’s interior access can change during restoration work, so check the official castle notice before visiting.

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  2. 02

    Sakae, Nagoya

    Oasis 21 & Hisaya-odori Park

    Oasis 21 & Hisaya-odori Park, Sakae, Nagoya, Japan

    A modern glass-and-water landmark opens onto the park boulevard. It makes a good reset before heading south and gives the day a distinctly contemporary Nagoya chapter.

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  3. 03

    Osu, Naka, Nagoya

    Osu Kannon & Osu Shopping District

    Osu Kannon & Osu Shopping District, Osu, Naka, Nagoya, Japan

    Temple grounds, old shopping arcades, independent shops and street food sit close together here. Leave room to follow a side street rather than trying to cover every lane.

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  4. 04

    Atsuta, Nagoya

    Atsuta Jingu

    Atsuta Jingu, Atsuta, Nagoya, Japan

    The broad shrine grounds feel a world away from the arcades. Walk respectfully, follow posted photography guidance and check the shrine’s visitor information for current access.
