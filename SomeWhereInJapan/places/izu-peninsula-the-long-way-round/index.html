Izu Peninsula, the long way round

region · JAPAN

Take the coastal road for cliff walks, fishing harbors, beaches, and hot springs on a peninsula made for a slower itinerary.

NatureSlow travel

Explore the route

Izu makes more sense as a set of bases than as one hurried loop. The official national tourism guide separates eastern resort towns such as Atami and Ito from Shimoda in the south, the rugged capes, and the peninsula’s inland highlands; it advises allowing several days to explore.

THE ROUTE · 5 STOPS

Izu Peninsula, the long way round: a suggested exploration sequence

Pick one base—such as Atami, Ito or Shimoda—and build a focused day around that area. The JNTO guide identifies the Shinkansen route from Tokyo to Atami, but this does not make the far-south or west-coast sights a same-day extension.
