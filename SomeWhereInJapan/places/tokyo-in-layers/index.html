01 · East Tokyo

temple lanes to market streets

Temple lanes, Ueno’s park and lively market streets.

Begin among Asakusa’s temple approaches and side streets, then continue north to Ueno’s park, museums and Ameyoko market lanes. Keep the two neighborhoods as one east-side day and choose your museum or park stop based on the time you have.

Stops

  1. 01

    Asakusa & Senso-ji

    Senso-ji, Asakusa, Taito City, Tokyo, Japan

    Walk the Kaminarimon–Nakamise approach, then leave room for the smaller lanes around the temple precinct.

  2. 02

    Ueno Park & Ameyoko

    Ueno Park and Ameyoko, Taito City, Tokyo, Japan

    Choose a museum or a slower park loop, then finish among Ameyoko’s market lanes. Museum schedules differ; check the venue you choose.

Getting to Tokyo

Haneda and Narita connect to central Tokyo by rail, bus and taxi. Within the city, JR, Tokyo Metro, Toei Subway and private railways serve the main districts. Choose an arrival station based on your first route.
