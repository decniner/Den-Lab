A PLACE-LED FIELD GUIDE

Hakone through the mist

Approximate area reference · attraction

Onsen ryokan, cedar avenues, and lake views beneath the cloud-wrapped Hakone hills.

See the place map ↓Plan your arrival

Hakone through the mist is best understood as a journey across the mountain, not a cluster of nearby stops. Start at the Hakone Open-Air Museum, where sculpture sits in a landscape of hills and gardens, then climb from Gora by cable car and ropeway toward Owakudani. The volcanic valley makes the earth itself the spectacle: steam rises from the ground, and the view opens toward the surrounding caldera. Continue down to Togendai, where the sightseeing cruise crosses Lake Ashi to Motohakone; from the port, a walk leads to Hakone Shrine beneath cedar trees and to the lakeside torii. The sequence follows Hakone's linked transport rather than trying to drive between every viewpoint. June to mid-July brings hydrangeas along the mountain railway and cable-car route, while clear days offer the best chance of seeing Mount Fuji. Ropeway, cruise and road operations can change with weather, so check service notices before setting out. Allow roughly 7 to 9 hours as a guide estimate; admission is separate at museums and some attractions, and the full circuit rewards an early start.

GETTING THERE

Choose the right arrival

Train: From Shinjuku, the Odakyu Romancecar runs directly to Hakone-Yumoto; its limited-express ticket is separate from the Hakone Freepass. From Tokyo Station, take the Tokaido Shinkansen or JR Tokaido Line to Odawara, then Hakone Tozan Railway to Hakone-Yumoto. Bus: Highway buses connect Shinjuku and airports to Hakone; local Hakone Tozan buses link the lake, shrine and Hakone-Yumoto. Air: Haneda and Narita access is by rail or airport bus onward to Hakone; there is no airport in the area. Car: use the Tomei Expressway via Odawara or Gotemba and local roads, but mountain roads and parking can be slow in busy periods. The Hakone Freepass covers designated transport only; check current inclusions and service status.

Start at Hakone-Yumoto and ride the Hakone Tozan train to the Open-Air Museum. Return to Gora, take the cable car and ropeway to Owakudani, then continue by ropeway to Togendai. Cross Lake Ashi by sightseeing cruise to Motohakone-ko and walk to Hakone Shrine. The official one-day course follows this order; this guide allows 7 to 9 hours, including a slower museum visit and connections. Return by bus from Motohakone or extend the stay overnight.

WHERE TO STAY

Hakone-Yumoto — The easiest base for Odakyu and Hakone Tozan connections, baggage services and an early start.

Gora — A convenient overnight base for the Open-Air Museum, mountain railway, cable car and onsen stays.

Motohakone — Stay by Lake Ashi to enjoy the shrine and shoreline without rushing for the last bus.

FEES & VISIT TIMEMuseum admission is separate. The Romancecar requires a separate limited-express surcharge; confirm current prices, pass validity and weather-related suspensions.
