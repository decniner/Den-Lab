A PLACE-LED FIELD GUIDE

Cape Ashizuri’s ocean edge

Approximate area reference · attraction

Stand above the Pacific at Shikoku’s southern tip, where lighthouse walks meet wild sea air.

See the place map ↓Plan your arrival

At Cape Ashizuri, Shikoku seems to end in open water. The lighthouse stands above cliffs where the Pacific horizon stretches around the headland, and the coastal path alternates between exposed viewpoints and subtropical greenery. Nearby Kongofukuji Temple is the 38th temple on the Shikoku pilgrimage, adding a human and spiritual scale to the ocean spectacle. Walk the marked loop slowly; wind, spray and sudden rain can make exposed rock dangerous. The cape lies far from major rail hubs, so the journey is part of the visit rather than a quick detour. Spring and autumn bring more comfortable conditions, while summer is hot and typhoon season can disrupt travel. Stay overnight if possible, arrive with fuel and supplies, and let the last light linger over the sea. Cape Ashizuri Park. Begin at the visitor area and follow signed paths to the cliffs; carry water and keep away from unstable edges. Cape Ashizuri Lighthouse. View the active lighthouse from permitted public paths; access inside is not generally part of the visit. Kongofukuji Temple. Visit the 38th Shikoku pilgrimage temple above the sea, observing worship etiquette and posted access rules. John Manjiro statue viewpoint. Add the nearby coastal monument if daylight and transport allow; return by the same safe road route.

GETTING THERE

Choose the right arrival

From Nakamura Station on the Tosa Kuroshio Railway, local buses take roughly 1 hour 40 minutes to the cape; check the sparse timetable carefully. Kochi Ryoma Airport connects to Kochi City, then rail and bus continue south. Drivers use Route 321 and signed cape parking; roads are long and winding. A car is useful but requires careful daylight planning.

Walk the cape's marked viewpoints and lighthouse area, then visit Kongofukuji before returning to town. Allow 3-5 hours on site and most of a day for transport from Nakamura. Do not approach cliff edges in high wind or wet weather.
