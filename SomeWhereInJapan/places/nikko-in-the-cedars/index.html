Nikko in
the cedars

A World Heritage walk from Tobu-Nikko Station

A red bridge marks the threshold. Beyond it, cedar shade gives way to temple halls, a lavish shogun's mausoleum, and the intricate craft of Nikko's sacred precinct.

Explore the route

Nikko's most memorable day is best understood as a sequence, not a checklist. Start at the river crossing, then move through the neighbouring religious precincts at a pace that leaves room to look up: at carved gates, painted eaves, lanterns, and the immense trees that hold the sites together.

THE WALK - FIVE STOPS

A route through the sacred precinct

Begin at Tobu-Nikko Station and take the World Heritage Sightseeing Bus to Shinkyo. Continue on foot through the linked temple and shrine grounds. Return to the station by bus from the Taiyuin/Futarasan-jinja stop. Select a numbered stop to highlight it on the map; each leg opens current directions.

WHY THIS ORDER

From threshold to inner precinct

Shinkyo makes the approach visible: a sacred bridge at the entrance to the mountain shrines. Rinnoji brings the Buddhist story into focus through Sanbutsudo and its three Buddha images. Toshogu shifts the scale toward vivid, intricate craftsmanship and the memory of Tokugawa Ieyasu. Nearby Futarasan-jinja connects the precinct to Nikko's three sacred mountains. Finish at Taiyuin, Tokugawa Iemitsu's mausoleum, where monumental gates and a quieter register of ornament reward an unhurried final visit.

The stop sequence follows the Nikko Official Guide's walking highlights through the World Heritage shrines and temples, with Shinkyo as the approach and Taiyuin as the final precinct stop.

GETTING THERE

Two rail approaches from Tokyo

Tobu route: Limited express trains run from Asakusa to Tobu-Nikko in about two hours. Tobu Railway describes the SPACIA route at approximately 1 hour 50 minutes. Train schedules and fares vary; check the operator before booking.

JR route: Take the Tohoku Shinkansen from Tokyo or Ueno to Utsunomiya, then change to the JR Nikko Line for Nikko Station. JNTO gives about 1 hour 40 minutes total. The mapped route starts at Tobu-Nikko Station; the two stations are separate, nearby origins.

At Tobu-Nikko Station, use the World Heritage Sightseeing Bus to Shinkyo. The official Nikko guide lists approximately five minutes from either Nikko station to the Shinkyo bus stop. The return leg is via the Taiyuin/Futarasan-jinja bus stop. Check the current Tobu bus timetable and stop notices on the day.

TICKETS & OPENING

Check the paid precincts before you go

Admission is separate across operators. The Rinnoji Pass covers Sanbutsudo Hall and Taiyuin for ¥900 for adults; Toshogu charges separately. Futarasan's main shrine grounds are free for worship visits, with separate fees for specific areas. The Shinkyo crossing itself is separately ticketed. Prices can change, so confirm directly before travel.

  • Nikko Toshogu: Visit Tochigi lists ¥1,600 adult admission, 9:00 - 17:00 April - October and 9:00 - 16:00 November - March, with last entry 30 minutes before closing. Check current Toshogu information.
  • Rinnoji / Taiyuin: Rinnoji lists ¥400 Sanbutsudo, ¥550 Taiyuin, and ¥900 for the two-site pass. Check current hours and fees.
  • Shinkyo Bridge: Nikko Official Guide lists a ¥300 crossing ticket. Check access and ticket details.
  • Futarasan-jinja: The main shrine area is open for worship without charge; the garden has a separate admission. Check visitor information.

Do not treat listed hours as a guarantee of access to every hall. Festivals, repair work and special closures can affect entry. Check the official operator page for each site before leaving.
