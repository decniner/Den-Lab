PLACE-SPECIFIC PLANNING · Tohoku

Plan your visit to Oirase stream walk

This is a one-way gorge walk, following the published order from Nenokuchi to Yakeyama. Allow roughly five hours for the full 14 km, or use the shorter 9 km Ishigedo–Nenokuchi section (about 2.5 hours). Published segment times vary by source and walking conditions.

Stops in the published order

  1. 01 · Nenokuchi lakeside trailhead

    The Lake Towada-side gateway to the gorge walk. The JR Bus Nenokuchi stop is a clear mapped access point; confirm the current bus timetable before choosing a one-way walk.

  2. 02 · Choshi Otaki Falls

    The largest named waterfall on this route and a natural early highlight. The official walking map places it about 1.5 km and 30 minutes from Nenokuchi; the Tohoku route lists a shorter 20-minute segment, so allow for pace and conditions.

  3. 03 · Kumoi-no-Nagare viewpoint access

    A turbulent, fast-moving section of the stream. The map point is the named JR Bus stop used as the access marker, beside the official walking route.

  4. 04 · Kumoi-no-Taki Falls

    A waterfall framed by the gorge’s deep green forest. The official route continues here from Kumoi-no-Nagare; stay on the signed path and use the footbridge viewpoints.

  5. 05 · Makado-iwa viewpoint access

    A dramatic rock formation beside the stream. Its map point is the named JR Bus stop, serving as the access marker for the viewpoint.

  6. 06 · Ishigedo rest house and trail stop

    A useful rest and trail-access point along the gorge. The Oirase map identifies facilities and a bus stop here; check service dates and return options before walking onward.

  7. 07 · Yakeyama gorge entrance

    The inland end of this one-way walk and the southern gateway to the gorge. The full Nenokuchi–Yakeyama section is about 14 km and around five hours on the official walking map.
