PLACE-SPECIFIC PLANNING · Kansai

Plan your visit to Koyasan’s lantern forest

A daylight visit through cedar, stone memorials and lantern light. The route is a quiet approach to a sacred place, not a night walk; use the official guided tour for after-dark access.

Mapped order · 4 stops

  1. 01 · Ichinohashi Bridge

    Begin at the traditional entrance and take a moment to read the etiquette signs.

  2. 02 · Cedar and memorial avenue

    Follow the marked path through the forest; the memorials span centuries and reflect many parts of Japanese history.

  3. 03 · Miroku-ishi approach

    A distinctive stone feature along the route; observe from the permitted path and do not disturb offerings.

  4. 04 · Torodo Lantern Hall

    The hall is normally open 6:00–17:30. Check current notices; the mausoleum itself is restricted.
