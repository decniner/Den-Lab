Kumamoto Castle

A castle visit shaped by recovery. The approach, stone walls and visible restoration work tell a story that is still unfolding; access routes can change as repairs continue.

Kumamoto Castle’s stone walls show the force of the 2016 earthquakes and the scale of the work to restore them. Follow the designated visitor corridor toward the keep, then take time at Ninomaru to see how the hilltop fortress sits above the city. Entry gates can vary by day.
