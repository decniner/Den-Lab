  1. 01

    Ritsurincho, Takamatsu

    Ritsurin Garden

    Ritsurin Garden, Ritsurincho, Takamatsu, Japan

    Paths loop around ponds, islands and borrowed views of the hills. Enter early if you want the garden’s quieter light; check seasonal opening hours and gate access.

  2. 02

    Takamatsu city centre

    Sanuki udon lunch

    Sanuki udon lunch, Takamatsu city centre, Japan

    Make lunch part of the plan with the region’s famous wheat noodles. Many specialist shops keep short hours or sell out, so check the day’s opening before choosing a stop.

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  3. 03

    Gyokocho, Takamatsu

    Tamamo Park · Takamatsu Castle site

    Tamamo Park · Takamatsu Castle site, Gyokocho, Takamatsu, Japan

    The former castle’s moat opens directly toward the harbour. Its waterside setting makes the site feel distinct from inland castle towns; check the park’s current opening information.

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  4. 04

    Kitahama, Takamatsu

    Kitahama Alley

    Kitahama Alley, Kitahama, Takamatsu, Japan

    A cluster of renovated port warehouses now holds small shops and cafés. It is a gentle final stop before returning to the station or extending the evening by the water.
