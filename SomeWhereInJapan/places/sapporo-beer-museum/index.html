  1. 01

    Central Sapporo

    Sapporo Clock Tower

    This wooden hall began as the drill hall of Sapporo Agricultural College. Step inside to see how a new frontier city took shape; time your visit near the hour for the clock chime.

    30 min suggested visit

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  2. 02

    Sapporo

    Nijo Market

    Sapporo grew around the fishing and farming communities of the Ishikari region. Nijo Market still makes a lively morning stop for seafood, produce, and a close look at Hokkaido ingredients. Stall hours vary.

    40 min suggested visit

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  3. 03

    Odori 1-chome

    Sapporo TV Tower

    From the observation deck, Odori Park reads as a long green axis through central Sapporo, with the city and distant mountains beyond it. Check visibility before paying for the view.

    40 min suggested visit

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  4. 04

    Central Sapporo

    Odori Park

    Odori is both a downtown park and a civic stage. Walk its broad blocks to see fountains, flower beds, and seasonal events; the character changes with the festival calendar.

    40 min suggested visit

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  5. 05

    Susukino

    Ganso Sapporo Ramen Yokocho

    N Grande Building 1F, 3-8 Minami 5-jo Nishi, Chuo-ku, Sapporo

    This compact Susukino alley is known for small ramen counters serving Sapporo-style miso bowls. Pick a shop that is open when you arrive, since hours and queues differ.

    40 min suggested visit

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  6. 06

    Central Sapporo

    Tanuki Koji Shopping Street

    A covered arcade threads through central Sapporo, where generations-old shops sit beside new cafes and game halls. Wander a few blocks and notice how the street changes from one section to the next.

    60 min suggested visit

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
  7. 07

    Higashi Ward

    Sapporo Beer Museum and Beer Garden

    The red-brick former factory tells the story of a beer brand that helped define Sapporo. The adjoining garden is a separate way to spend the evening; check current museum hours and dining reservations.

    180–240 min suggested visit

    Open this stop in OpenStreetMap ↗
