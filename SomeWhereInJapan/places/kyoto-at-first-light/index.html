A CLOSER LOOK

Why this place stays with you

At Fushimi Inari, the first gate is only the beginning. Follow the torii uphill until the crowd thins and the city begins to appear between the trees. You do not need to reach the summit to feel the mountain change pace; turn back when the climb stops fitting your morning. Tofukuji brings the route back to architectural scale, with deep eaves, broad grounds and a maple ravine that glows in autumn. Finish at Sennyuji, where spacious approaches and imperial history create a quieter close. Together, these stops make a morning of contrasts: movement and stillness, familiar icons and the quieter spaces between them. Build in time for temple hours, which differ from Fushimi Inari’s all-hours grounds.

GETTING THERE

Choose the right arrival

From Kyoto Station take the JR Nara Line to Inari Station; the shrine entrance is outside. Keihan Fushimi-Inari is nearby. Tofukuji and Sennyuji are reached by JR or Keihan trains and short walks. Kansai Airport connects by JR Haruka; Itami uses airport bus and rail.

Enter Fushimi Inari at first light, then take rail or walk toward Tofukuji and finish at Sennyuji. Allow 5–7 hours; opening times affect the order.
