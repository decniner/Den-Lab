# Cute Japan Adventures — KDP pilot launch brief

**Prepared:** 25 October 2026  
**Product:** *Cute Japan Adventures: A Coloring Book for Curious Kids*  
**Format:** 8.5 × 11 in paperback, 64 pages, black ink on white paper, no interior bleed  
**Interior:** 30 original Japan-inspired scenes, each followed by a blank reverse page; 4 opening pages

## What is built

- `cute-japan-adventures-interior.pdf` — 64-page print interior at 8.5 × 11 in. The drawings are vector line art with large areas intended for younger colorists.
- `cute-japan-adventures-cover.pdf` — full paperback wrap for 64 pages, black ink/white paper, with 0.125 in cover bleed and a reserved barcode area. It has no spine lettering because KDP only prints spine text on books with more than 79 pages.
- `build_coloring_book.py` — source used to generate both PDFs; edit this before changing page count, trim, or paper because those changes can invalidate the cover dimensions.

**Title:** Cute Japan Adventures

**Description**

Take a little coloring trip around Japan. Meet a friendly shiba, follow a trail of red shrine gates, visit a lantern-lit festival, and spot Mt. Fuji, a bullet train, a lucky cat and favorite Japanese foods.

Inside are 30 original, Japan-inspired scenes with bold outlines and room to add your own colors. Every picture has a blank reverse page, making this book easy to use with crayons and colored pencils and giving young artists space to draw their own ideas.

A cheerful activity for curious kids, family travel days, rainy afternoons and anyone who loves discovering Japan.

**What's inside**
- 30 original scenes inspired by places, animals, food and traditions in Japan
- 8.5 × 11 inch large-format pages
- Single-sided illustrations with a blank page behind each one
- Simple, open compositions for younger colorists

Do not promise markers will never bleed through paper; the blank reverse page reduces the risk of marking the next illustration, but paper performance varies by marker.

## Unit economics (estimate, not a sales forecast)

For the US marketplace, KDP's current black-ink price table lists a **US$2.84 fixed print cost** for large-trim books with 24–110 pages. Its royalty rate is 60% at a list price of US$9.99 or above. Therefore:

`(US$9.99 × 60%) − US$2.84 = US$3.154`, or about **US$3.15 per shipped copy before advertising, tax, refunds/adjustments, or other business costs**.

| Monthly copies | Estimated royalty before other costs |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | $0 |
| 10 | $31.54 |
| 50 | $157.70 |
| 100 | $315.40 |
| 1,000 | $3,154.00 |

This table is arithmetic only. It does not imply that any sales volume is likely. At this unit margin, paid acquisition can erase the royalty quickly: an average advertising cost above about $3.15 per sale makes each attributed copy unprofitable before tax and overhead. Confirm the print cost, royalty, local tax treatment, and customer price in KDP's calculator and title setup; KDP can change rates and costs.

## Go-to-market pilot

This is a visual and publishing prototype, not a proven publishing business or a finished commercial edition. A quick current web scan found existing Japan-themed coloring titles, including a multi-volume prefecture series and books positioned for children. “Japan coloring book” alone is not a defensible niche. The current interior also uses deliberately simple vector sketches and needs illustration-quality review before any sale. Do not build a large catalog from the same untested idea.

**Differentiation to test before publishing:** rebuild the book around one recurring original travel companion and a clear child-facing learning hook, such as one carefully checked Japan fact or word per scene. The current prototype does not yet deliver that differentiator; it is a proposed next edit, not a claim about this edition.

1. **Validate the shelf.** On Amazon.com, inspect the first 20 relevant results for kids' Japan/Japan-travel coloring books. Record price, page count, review count and date, preview quality, and visible product angle. Reviews and rank are imperfect signals; they do not prove a book's unit sales.
2. **Test the product with real parents.** Show 5–8 interior pages and the cover to 8–10 parents with children in the intended age range. Ask what the child would color first, which pages feel too detailed, whether the pages feel worth the proposed price, and what they expected but did not see. Do not ask only whether they “like” it.
3. **Correct production defects.** Check the complete PDF at full size, including line joins, page order, margins, clipping, spelling, scene variety, and whether each reverse is blank. Order a physical proof and inspect line thickness, binding clearance, contrast, trim, and cover color before enabling sale.
4. **Publish one pilot.** Add the book in KDP, select paperback, black ink, white paper, 8.5 × 11 trim, no bleed, and the final page count. Disclose AI-generated content in KDP's content questionnaire. Upload the interior and cover, then inspect KDP's Print Previewer and resolve every warning before submitting.
5. **Use organic discovery first.** Post short page-turn clips and show the book being colored. Track views, clicks, and orders separately. Avoid buying reviews, asking family to post incentivized reviews, or claiming sales evidence you cannot substantiate.
6. **Set a decision gate before spending.** After 30 days live, review impressions, product-page visits, conversion, orders, refunds, and royalties. Revise the cover/listing if people reach the page but do not buy. If there is little qualified traffic, the issue is discovery; a new interior alone will not solve it. Only commission ads after a conservative estimate of expected cost per sale is below the actual royalty.

## Risks and publishing checks

- **Demand is unproven.** Competitor pricing, rankings, and review counts do not tell us how many copies their books sell today. The TikTok's “22,000 sold” claim is not independently verified here.
- **The current art is a prototype.** These scenes are generated as programmatic vector illustrations. Review them page by page for artistic quality, visual balance, cultural accuracy, and age suitability. A physical proof is essential before launch.
- **AI disclosure.** KDP requires publishers to disclose AI-generated text, images, or translations. The book includes a reminder; complete KDP's disclosure workflow accurately for the content.
- **Rights and originality.** Do not use a competitor's screenshot as an art-generation reference or ask a tool to reproduce a recognizable book, character, layout, or signature style. This prototype uses original generic Japan-inspired scenes and no third-party source artwork.
- **Metadata accuracy.** Do not claim “educational,” “official,” “best-selling,” or “teacher-approved” unless substantiated. Use categories and search terms that accurately describe the book.
- **Japan marketplace economics differ.** Amazon.co.jp currently uses yen pricing, its own royalty thresholds, and a different large-trim print cost. This pilot's US-dollar estimate is only for Amazon.com.
