Career Decisions & Schedule

Page 3

Decisions Regarding Post-Graduation Paths

  • Public High Schools Only: Attend school briefings and individual consultations for target schools.
  • Private High Schools Only: Attend individual consultations with your report card (Tsuchisho) and test results. Decide between Single Choice (Tantan — applying to 1 school as primary) or Concurrent Choice (Heigan — applying as backups).
  • 1st Choice Public + Backup Private (Concurrent): Attend briefings for public schools and reserve individual consultations for private high schools. Note: You may only apply to 1 public high school.
  • Other Options (Vocational / Employment): Consult with your homeroom teacher or local Public Employment Security Offices (Hello Work).

Upcoming Schedule (October 2026 – March 2027)

  • February / March 2027: Public Application Changes (Feb 17–18), Public Written Exam (Feb 25), Public Interview (Feb 26), Results Announcement (March 5).

Public High School Exam Policies (Reiwa 9)

Page 5

Basic Policies for Question Preparation

  • Curriculum Alignment: Questions are based on standard junior high school curriculum guidelines.
  • Skill Assessment: Tests fundamental knowledge, problem-solving, thinking, judgment, and expression skills needed to solve problems.
  • Question Format Balance: A balanced mix of question formats ensuring fair assessment across subjects.

Answer Format Breakdown

  • OMR Mark-Sheet Questions: Account for approximately 90% of total score points across core subjects.
  • Written / Short-Answer Questions: Account for approximately 10% of total score points.

Public Exam Changes & Electronic System

Page 6

Changes for Reiwa 9 Public Admissions

  • Mark-Sheet Format Introduced: Standardized bubble sheet answering system for all 5 subjects (Japanese, Social Studies, Math, Science, English).
  • Interviews for All Schools: All public high schools will conduct interviews.
  • Evaluation Report (Chosasho) Streamlining: Record format simplified; attendance scoring rules updated.

Electronic Application System (Denshi Shukkon)

Applications for Saitama Prefectural Public High Schools are submitted online using a computer, smartphone, or tablet, alongside access to a printer.

An operational test/practice run will be conducted in November–December for applicants and junior high schools.

Student Evaluation Reports (Chosasho)

Page 7

Chosasho Formats

  • Public High Schools: Standardized prefectural format.
  • Private High Schools: Standard format or school-specific individual formats.

Grading Scale & Score Calculation

  • Evaluation Scale: Relative 5-point grading scale across 9 subjects.
  • Yearly Calculation: 5 points × 9 subjects = 45 points per year.
  • 3-Year Total: 45 points × 3 years = 135 total points.
  • Grade 3 scores combine performance from Term 1 and Term 2.

Verification of Report Items

Page 8

Verification Items for Private High School Standard Formats

  • Special Activity Records: Verify committee/class duty roles for Grades 1, 2, and 3 (select 1 primary role per year if multiple exist). Confirm school awards and award years.
  • Other Records: Verify qualifications (Eiken, Kanji Kentei, Abacus, etc.), volunteer work, and external sports/cultural achievements.
  • Corrections: Mark missing items or additions in red pen on the verification form.

Private Admissions & Nov Consultations

Page 10

Private High School Recruitment Categories

  • Single Choice (Tantan): School is applicant's top choice. Enrollment is mandatory upon passing.
  • Concurrent Choice (Heigan): Applicant applies to private school as a backup while aiming for a public high school.
  • Recommendation (Suisen): Based on principal recommendation, self-recommendation, or scholarship status meeting school criteria.
  • General Admission (Ippan): For applicants who do not meet recommendation thresholds; selection depends purely on high exam scores.

Focus Areas for November Three-Way Consultations

  1. Target School Selection: Reviewing attendance at school briefings/consultations and evaluating passing probability.
  2. Confirming Application Types: Finalizing Public vs. Private route, Single vs. Concurrent choices, and target course tracks.
  3. Document Verification: Confirming details in the Student Evaluation Report (Chosasho) and eligibility for recommendation letters.

Dec Consultations & Guidance Advice

Page 11

December Three-Way Consultations (Optional)

  • Finalizing Choice of High Schools: Specifying 1st choice school/course and backup choices.
  • Confirming Application Procedures & Schedules: Re-confirming details in the Student Evaluation Report.
  • December 24: Distribution of the "Notification Record of Academic Performance and Activities" to families.

Advice for Students

  • Treasure School Classes: Nothing taught in class is ever wasted!
  • Aim to Overcome Weaknesses: Reducing weak areas is just as important as building strengths.
  • Set Clear Goals: Decide specific targets with clear deadlines (e.g., "I will reach this score by this date!").
  • Maintain a Regular Routine: Wake up early to study rather than staying up late.
  • Establish Fixed Study Habits: Study 2 hours daily or set a fixed start time (e.g., starting at 9:00 PM).
  • Gather Accurate Career Information: Understand your own educational path better than anyone else.

Kawagoe Municipal "Community Special Selection"

To 3rd Grade Students and Parents Residing in Kawagoe City:
Regarding the "Community Special Selection" for Kawagoe Municipal High School (Reiwa 9 Saitama Public High School Admissions)
From: Principal, Kawagoe Municipal High School

1. Purpose

Promote community-rooted education by accepting students with a strong sense of purpose who excel in culture, sports, student council, club activities, or certifications, nurturing individuals who take pride in Kawagoe City.

2. Student Acceptance Policy

  • Students with sufficient academic capability and strong drive to learn.
  • Active participants in school events and club activities.
  • Students with good lifestyle habits who respect rules.
  • Clear motivation regarding their chosen course of study.

3. Application Eligibility

Students selecting Kawagoe Municipal High School as 1st choice, residing in Kawagoe City, with proven achievements or talent in academics, sports, culture, or student council.

4. Required Documents

  • Community Special Selection Statement of Purpose (地域特別選抜志願書)
  • Certificate of Application Eligibility (出願資格証明書) issued by junior high school principal.

5. Quota

Approximately 10% of total recruitment capacity (~28 students out of 280 planned total).
