# Premium documentary short

Render the actual video and save the production files. Adapt the topic and editorial tone to the user's message; do not reuse the previous finance script for unrelated subjects. Read [production mechanics](references/production.md) before adapting the renderer. For financial content, also read [financial accuracy](references/finance.md).

## Production defaults

- Exactly 80 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 with 48 kHz AAC. Explicit user specifications override these defaults.
- Microsoft Edge `en-US-AndrewNeural`, calm, mature, authoritative, natural pacing. Generate and measure narration before locking visual timing. Aim for roughly 165–185 words, but prioritize measured natural delivery; the working example needed 194 words. Adjust wording rather than accelerating speech or padding with long silence. A brief closing hold after complete speech is acceptable.
- Premium documentary aesthetic: dark navy, warm white, restrained gold, purposeful negative space, clear visual hierarchy. Blend relevant realistic moving footage with original diagrams, charts, counters, and animated typography. Make a meaningful visual change approximately every 2–4 seconds without chaotic editing.
- Large stable captions: bold 64–76 px, white inactive text, the currently spoken word gold, maximum two short lines. Keep titles, graphics, essential assumptions and captions in separate regions. Use a dedicated dark caption backing above bottom controls and clear of right-side icons. Essential assumptions must remain readable on a phone.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the existing working tools, narration configuration and assets without exposing credentials. Preserve earlier exports; create a clearly named project or revision folder.
2. Consider three truthful opening hooks internally and select the strongest. Write a coherent script with short natural sentences, evidence appropriate to the topic, and a memorable ending. No introduction or logo before the hook. Verify material claims using authoritative sources and record URLs/access dates.
3. Generate Andrew narration with Edge word-boundary events. Restore punctuation only after checking that every timing token matches the authored script. Reuse unchanged narration for a visual-only revision. If Andrew fails, troubleshoot the free setup and reusable audio before using another legitimate free English voice; disclose the substitution.
4. Select varied, relevant licensed moving shots. Inspect the downloaded footage, actual motion, duration, resolution, framing and license. Do not loop a small pool of clips, use unrelated stock, or pass animated stills off as moving footage. Use restrained footage dissolves independently of typography. Original motion graphics may legitimately replace unavailable free footage; disclose that substitution.
5. Build the edit around measured narration. Animate quantities from calculated data, not manually entered chart endpoints. Keep complete caption phrases visible through word gaps. Use one caption phrase layer at a time: do not crossfade old and new phrase text into overlapping double exposure. The active word changes color without moving or fading the phrase.
6. Render locally with installed FFmpeg/Python tools where practical. Reuse the free setup and properly licensed assets. Do not buy credits, activate trials/subscriptions, create billable resources, bypass controls or use unlicensed media. If a preferred paid tool is unavailable, continue with a legitimate free alternative. If a new charge is genuinely necessary, stop before incurring it and state the exact cost and reason. Keep unrelated projects and credentials intact.
7. Review the exported video, fix detected issues and render again. Do not infer quality from encoder success. Follow the checks below and disclose any that cannot be performed.

## Quality gate

- Verify runtime within one frame, dimensions, frame rate and full decoded frame count. Decode the complete video and audio; check clipping, unexpected silence, broken assets and blank frames.
- Check authored text bounds and intersections on every frame. Inspect actual exported frames at every scene and caption change, speech gaps, the opening and ending. Review the full chronological sequence and phone-size previews for hierarchy, readability, obstruction and safe zones.
- Check actual exported caption visibility and gold pixels against timing events. Compare the original narration and encoded audio for synchronization or drift. These checks do not independently prove phonetic alignment.
- Compare separated exported frames within moving shots to detect frozen tails or looping. Respect source resolution; a full-HD canvas does not establish native full-HD source footage.
- Independently recalculate displayed financial quantities and rounding when relevant.
- Watch and listen to the complete final export when playback is available. If unavailable, explicitly leave subjective intelligibility/prosody, independent phonetic verification and real-device/older-viewer checks unverified. Do not label the entire requested quality gate passed when mandatory checks remain unavailable.

## Delivery

Save the MP4, narration, script, word timings and timed captions, calculation code/results where applicable, sources and asset-license notes, reproducible rendering instructions, three accurate titles, a short description, five hashtags, and a concise free-alternatives/unmet-requirements record. Music is optional: use a suitable license and keep it beneath speech, or omit it and disclose the omission.

Provide a clickable local MP4 link, its exact absolute path and filename, and the project location. State relevant verification results and limitations. Do not upload publicly without explicit authorization.
