# Cinematic animated documentary

Produce the complete narrated MP4 and reproducible project. Read [production mechanics](references/production.md) before synthesis or rendering. Explicit user specifications override these defaults.

## Defaults

- Full-length 1920x1080, 16:9, 30 fps, H.264/yuv420p, AAC at 48 kHz. Duration follows the entire script and measured narration; there is no 80-second limit.
- Local Kokoro `bm_george`, British English, mature restrained documentary delivery. Target roughly 125–140 spoken words per minute, excluding deliberate pauses. Measure the take; adjust native synthesis speed rather than accelerating recorded audio. Adapt tone to the subject.
- Dark charcoal, white technical linework, muted cyan, amber warnings and selective deep red. Combine actual progressive pen strokes, understandable causal diagrams and cinematic architectural illustrations. Meaningful animation follows spoken concepts; movement alone is insufficient.
- Chapter headings and selective explanatory typography. Supply a complete separate SRT. Do not inherit continuous white-and-gold karaoke captions from vertical-video skills; burn full captions only if requested.
- Original or appropriately licensed music and effects, ducked beneath speech. Reuse free local tools. Paid services and uploading require separate explicit authorization.

## Execution

Preserve supplied narration verbatim and keep the original immutable. If the user requires an existing script and it is missing, request it rather than inventing passages. For newly requested scripts, write to the brief and verify factual claims; identify speculative illustrated scenarios clearly.

Create a dedicated project. Generate cached narration and actual word/phrase timing, then author one relevant visual beat per meaningful sentence. Derive chapter boundaries, reveals, causal changes, sound cues and subtitles from the same final audio timeline. Reuse narration for visual-only revisions.

Draw paths by cumulative distance, gate objects and labels by spoken mentions, and persist causal changes across sentence boundaries. Compose diagrams so moving flows represent real edges and destinations. Use restrained parallax and lighting for illustrated scenes. Inspect representative scenes before committing to full chapter renders.

Render chapters independently with validated checkpoints, assemble the full film, and run the checks in the production reference. Fix observed pauses, drift, collisions or semantic errors and recheck the actual revised export. Do not substitute a preview for the requested complete film.

## Delivery

Save the MP4, SRT, immutable script, narration, word timings, storyboard, source/assets, reproduction instructions and sources/licenses. Include a 1280x720 thumbnail, title, description, actual chapter timestamps and appropriate disclosure. Record runtime, word/scene/beat counts, render time, size and verification results.

Deliver a clickable MP4 link and exact path. Report full playback/listening, independent acoustic alignment and real-device review as unverified when unavailable; sampled frames and model timestamps do not establish those checks.
