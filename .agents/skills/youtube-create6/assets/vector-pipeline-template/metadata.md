# Description

A demonstration of a free local vector-animation pipeline: original marker strokes, recurring paper-cutout characters, money transfers, chart movement and synchronized George captions. The fictional USD example uses 5% simple annual interest on $10M for one year without fees or repayments. Principal and accrued interest are distinct. Illustrative chart prices are not forecasts. Inheritance comments refer to United States rules and include exceptions; basis may step up or down. This is educational content, not personalized financial/tax advice or an endorsement of securities-backed borrowing. Original assets/effects, local Kokoro bm_george. Sources and reproduction files accompany the video.
