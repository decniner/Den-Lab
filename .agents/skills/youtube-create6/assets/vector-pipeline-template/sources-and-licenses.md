# Sources and usage rights

Accessed 2026-10-09. This is a pipeline demonstration, educational content rather than personalized financial or tax advice. USD amounts, asset prices, loan eligibility, 5% rate and timeline are hypothetical. No historical-return claim, specific stock or guaranteed tax outcome is asserted.

- SEC/FINRA Investor Alert, securities-backed lines of credit: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/investor-28 — borrowing against collateral, maintenance calls, forced sales, lender terms and risks. An SBLOC and a margin account are different products. The demo narrates a maintenance call; “MARGIN CALL!” is an effect-catalogue example, not the name assigned to that loan.
- SEC Investor Bulletin, margin accounts: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-29 — margin-account calls and possible forced selling, supporting the terminology distinction.
- IRS gifts/inheritances FAQ: https://www.irs.gov/faqs/interest-dividends-other-types-of-income/gifts-inheritances/gifts-inheritances — inherited property basis generally tied to fair market value at death or permitted alternate valuation. Qualified, US-specific explanation only. Basis can move down as well as up; exceptions apply. The film therefore says “INHERITED BASIS”; requested “STEPPED-UP BASIS” is available in the marker catalogue for an appropriate example.
- IRS Publication 559: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p559 — inherited-basis exceptions and estate administration. Neither a tax elimination guarantee nor a claim that heirs personally owe every estate debt is made.
- SVG paths/stroke specification: https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG/paths.html — shared path geometry and distance-along-path stroke operations. The implementation exports progressive polyline prefixes using computed arc length, not a whole-line opacity fade.

All illustrated characters, original marker glyph geometry, diagrams and synthesized effects were authored for this pipeline. They may be reused with the supplied project (CC0 dedication for these original visual/audio assets). No external stock imagery, music, branded logos or third-party character likenesses are used. SVG exports include these original drawings and ordinary system-font text, not copied font outlines.

Segoe UI is an installed Windows font used for rasterized captions and ordinary annotations. Its binaries are not copied or redistributed. On another platform, use a locally licensed font and remeasure all text. Kokoro engine/model: https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro and https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0; the existing local model, George voice and license manifests are retained in the sibling Kokoro setup, not redistributed as a new model package. Free Pillow/NumPy/imageio-ffmpeg and FFmpeg runtimes are reused. No additional spending or public upload.

Math: `calculations.py` uses Decimal for principal x rate x years, verifies with separate integer arithmetic, and separates principal, accrued interest and total due. Numeric counters interpolate that result. Chart data are explicitly illustrative prices with no time horizon; they do not forecast growth or assert a universal collateral threshold. The interest fill is an explanatory counter, not observed account activity.
