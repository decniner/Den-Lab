# Full-screen cinematic documentary

Create the actual finished MP4 and reproducible project. Let realistic imagery and narration carry the story; text supports understanding. Read [production mechanics](references/production.md) before production. For financial topics, also read [financial accuracy](references/finance.md).

## Choose the requested visual mode

For cinematic documentary footage, follow the presentation contract below. For an explicitly requested illustrated, marker-drawn, cartoon or paper-cutout explainer, read [vector animation](references/vector-animation.md) and use the tested [pipeline template](assets/vector-pipeline-template/README.md). Important handwriting and figures are part of the explanation in this mode; do not impose a prior Earth episode's captions-only restriction on a new illustrated brief. Conversely, do not replace requested realistic Earth imagery with cartoons because this template is available. Explicit project format/duration/text instructions take precedence.

## Presentation contract

- Default: exactly 80 seconds, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 with 48 kHz AAC. Explicit user specifications override defaults.
- Fill the vertical canvas with relevant, high-quality realistic moving footage, detailed specimen imagery or credible reconstructions. Use cinematic composition, controlled camera motion and smooth transitions. Aim for a meaningful visual change every 2–4 seconds without chaotic editing or repetitive footage loops.
- Remove persistent mastheads, repeated scene headings, explanatory paragraphs, attribution strips, progress rules and boxed presentation panels. Use brief titles, important numbers and context labels only when they add information beyond the narration. Preserve readable scientific uncertainty, financial assumptions and necessary licensing attribution.
- Prefer genuine moving footage. Camera moves over photographs and static reconstruction plates are supplements, not a substitute silently described as fully moving footage. Match realism to the subject; inspect actual assets before choosing them. Label artistic interpretations, casts and modern analogues accurately.
- Full-screen means the imagery fills the canvas, not that every source must be aggressively cropped. Preserve important subjects above the caption region. Use another shot, a wider composition or a carefully blended peripheral extension when a portrait crop loses them. Inspect extensions for seams, streaks and stretched subjects.
- Use restrained dark navy, warm white and gold in typography and overlays; retain natural image color. Reserve separate regions for essential numbers/titles and captions.

## Voice and captions

Use free local Kokoro `bm_george`, British English, with deliberate natural delivery suited to the topic. Native synthesis speed around 0.92–0.98 is a starting point. Measure the take and rewrite naturally to fit. Preserve meaningful pauses and complete speech. Reuse unchanged narration for visual-only revisions. Do not impose angry financial-rant delivery on science, relationships or other topics.

Burn stable karaoke captions: bold 64–76 px, inactive words white, active word gold, maximum two short lines. Keep one complete phrase visible continuously, with fixed word positions; recolor at measured word events without blinking or word fades. Use a soft dark scrim for contrast. Keep captions above bottom controls, away from right-side icons and clear of important figures and other text.

## Production boundaries and delivery

Inspect and reuse the existing local setup. Create a new named project/revision folder and preserve prior exports. Select the strongest of three truthful hooks internally. Verify material claims using authoritative sources and save URLs, access dates and asset licenses.

Spend nothing: use licensed free assets and legitimate local tools. Install free dependencies only in permitted project locations. Never bypass licensing, authentication or usage limits. Troubleshoot George before a disclosed free voice substitution. Do not activate paid services, trials or resources; if a new charge is genuinely required, report its exact cost and reason before proceeding.

Run the export checks in the production reference, inspect actual output, fix defects and rerender. Report unavailable checks honestly; rendering success does not establish quality.

Deliver MP4, narration, script, word timings/timed captions, rendering files/instructions, sources/licenses, relevant calculations, three titles, description, five hashtags and substitutions/limitations. Provide a clickable MP4 link, exact filename/path and project location. Do not upload or publish unless separately authorized.
