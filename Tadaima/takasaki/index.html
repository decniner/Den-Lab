How the ranking works

One score out of 100, built from four parts — the same maths for every property, with the reasons printed on each card. No paid placement, no hidden weighting. A major caution (for example land in an urbanisation-control area, or a house sold as a renovation project) subtracts 7 points and is always shown, never buried.

Takasaki, in numbers

Takasaki (population ≈364,000 — Gunma's largest city, though the capital is Maebashi) is a bifurcating market: the centre concentrates while the fringe drifts. Used houses anchor at about ¥68万/tsubo city-wide (floor-area basis), flat for five years — the station area ≈¥85万, Tonyamachi ≈¥78万, Ino ≈¥70万, and the established western and southern fringe down at ¥42–51万, with pre-1981 stock pricing at land value. Land in-zone: ¥27万/tsubo at Takasaki (not the ~¥49万 all-use official average, which is station-front commercial), ¥26万 at Minami-Takasaki on official points, ¥20万 at Tonyamachi, then a quick step-down along the Joshin Line — Kita-Takasaki ¥18万, Kuragano ¥16万, Ino ¥15万, Shinmachi ¥12万 — to single figures on the western fringe. Walk distance barely moves prices here (~−4% at 10 minutes), so the walk bands on this page are deliberately gentle; what separates lots is zoning, not minutes.

Direction: official residential prices +0.5% in 2026 (commercial +1.3%), centre points up 3–4%, fringe down 1–2% — and the population is slowly shrinking (−2.5% in the 2025 Census), so demand support comes from concentration into the centre, not growth. Three standing cautions. The Tone and Karasu river systems flood: lowland cards (the Kuragano/Shinmachi belt, the Ino corridor, the Karasu side) need the hazard-map check. The Haruna foothills carry sediment-disaster zones on their slopes. And the fringe holds the classic regional trap — urbanisation-control and non-line-drawn land where rebuilding can be barred in principle; several lots in this collection sit in the control area and are scored only against control-zone comparables, with their status printed on the card rather than buried. Two honest negatives: Mt Haruna is a dormant volcano but no official volcanic hazard zone covers built-up Takasaki, and none is claimed; Mt Asama is another municipality's volcano, ~30 km west. Sources: MLIT transaction records and official land prices via Utinokati, Ekitan for train times.
