How the ranking works

One score out of 100, built from four parts — the same maths for every property, with the reasons printed on each card. No paid placement, no hidden weighting. A major caution (for example land in an urbanisation-control area, or a house sold as a renovation project) subtracts 7 points and is always shown, never buried.

PartPointsWhat it measures
Value45Land: price per tsubo against MLIT transaction averages for that station and walk band (via Utinokati). Houses: price per m² of floor area against the median for new / used houses in this collection.
Location30Walk minutes to the nearest station, plus a station tier — Machida (Odakyu / JR Yokohama Line hub) scores highest.
Space15Floor area for houses, plot size for land — with over-sized plots eased back, because very large usually means very restricted.
Confidence10Whether the facts were verified on the listing's own detail page, and how recent its information date is. Index-page finds score lower and say so.

Freshness chips: a green New this week chip marks a property added at the latest weekly check; a Price ↓ chip marks a verified price cut since the previous check, with the amount. Both clear at the next weekly check — they describe the last seven days, not the property.

Machida, in numbers

Machida (population 430,558, essentially flat for a decade) prices like a mature first-ring suburb. Used houses anchor at about ¥130万/tsubo city-wide (floor-area basis) — the centre (Morino/Haramachida/Nakamachi) ≈¥160万, the Kanamori belt toward Minami-Machida ≈¥148万, Naruse ≈¥140万 (a flagged proxy), Tamagawagakuen ≈¥128万 — with a documented age curve: ×0.83 at 10 years, ×0.70 at 20, ×0.60 at 30. Land at Machida station scores at ¥95万/tsubo for residential streets: neither the ¥181万 official station average (commercial-mixed — Haramachida's top commercial point prints over ¥1,000万) nor the ¥74万 raw catchment (a 20-minute average walk). Around it: Naruse ¥77万, Suzukakedai ¥80万 (a deliberate midpoint across an unresolved 25-point gap between its transaction and official series, flagged in the data), Tsukushino ¥75万, Tamagawagakuen-mae ¥62万, Tsurukawa ¥55万 in-zone, and Aihara ¥40万 at low confidence on 2–11 deals a year.

Direction: official residential prices +3.0% in 2026, centre-led with the fringe flat — no blanket uplift is applied anywhere on this page. Three standing cautions. Machida is Tama Hills country: sediment-disaster zones sit on its slopes and are checked lot by lot. The Sakai and Tsurumi river lowlands flood — including around Machida station itself and the southern lowland. And the northern and western districts hold urbanisation-control land where cheap ¥/tsubo is usually legal status, not value — rebuild status is printed wherever a listing discloses it.
