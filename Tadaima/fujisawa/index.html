How the ranking works

One score out of 100, built from four parts — the same maths for every property, with the reasons printed on each card. No paid placement, no hidden weighting. A major caution (for example land in an urbanisation-control area, or a house sold as a renovation project) subtracts 7 points and is always shown, never buried.

PartPointsWhat it measures
Value45Land: price per tsubo against MLIT transaction averages for that station and walk band (via Utinokati). Houses: price per m² of floor area against the median for new / used houses in this collection.
Location30Walk minutes to the nearest station, plus a station tier — Fujisawa (JR Tokaido / Odakyu / Enoden hub) scores highest.

Confidence10Whether the facts were verified on the listing's own detail page, and how recent its information date is. Index-page finds score lower and say so.

Freshness chips: a green New this week chip marks a property added at the latest weekly check; a Price ↓ chip marks a verified price cut since the previous check, with the amount. Both clear at the next weekly check — they describe the last seven days, not the property.

Fujisawa, in numbers

Fujisawa's land carries its houses: the age gradient is the shallowest on the coast because the shore districts price the plot, not the building. Used houses transact at about ¥158万/tsubo city-wide (floor-area basis): the Kugenuma district ~195, Tsujido ~187, the centre ~165, Honmachi ~147 and Zengyo ~143; by age, stock under 10 years runs ~183 and 30+-year stock still ~125. Land by station: Fujisawa, Tsujido and Hon-Kugenuma ~¥95万/tsubo (Fujisawa's all-use average of ~130 mixes station-front commercial — one Minami-Fujisawa point prints ~704 — and is not used), Shonandai ¥90万, Kugenuma-Kaigan and Yanagikoji ¥90万, Kugenuma ¥85万, Shonankaigan-koen ¥75万, Fujisawa-Honmachi ¥70万, then the inland step-down: Zengyo ¥60万, Mutsuai-Nichidai-mae ¥58万, Chogo ¥50万. Land falls ~35% by a 10-minute walk at the hub and ~45% by 16+ minutes city-wide; houses fall only ~19% over the same range.

Direction: strongly rising — 2026 公示地価 residential +5.4% (55 of 56 points up) after +5.0% in the 2025 基準地価， with the coast and hub stations rising about twice as fast as the northern Odakyu stations. Standing cautions. The Hikiji and Sakai river corridors flood — hazard-map checks on the lowland cards. The shore strip carries a tsunami hazard check, stated factually — it is simultaneously the premium strip, and no depths are guessed. The northern valleys and the Nishitomi/Daigiri scarp carry sediment-disaster zones, and the far north (Endo, Kuzuhara, Imada, Yoda) is an urbanisation-control fringe whose ¥2–27万/tsubo deals are never used as residential comparables. One researched negative: no evidenced fault runs under Fujisawa — the Kannawa/Kozu-Matsuda zone is western Kanagawa — so no fault caution is printed.
