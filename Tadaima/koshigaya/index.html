How the ranking works

One score out of 100, built from four parts — the same maths for every property, with the reasons printed on each card. No paid placement, no hidden weighting. A major caution (for example land in an urbanisation-control area, or a house sold as a renovation project) subtracts 7 points and is always shown, never buried.

PartPointsWhat it measures
Value45Land: price per tsubo against MLIT transaction averages for that station and walk band (via Utinokati). Houses: price per m² of floor area against the median for new / used houses in this collection.

Confidence10Whether the facts were verified on the listing's own detail page, and how recent its information date is. Index-page finds score lower and say so.

Freshness chips: a green New this week chip marks a property added at the latest weekly check; a Price ↓ chip marks a verified price cut since the previous check, with the amount. Both clear at the next weekly check — they describe the last seven days, not the property.

Koshigaya, in numbers

Koshigaya is two markets sharing a name: the priced-up twin hub and Laketown in the south, and the older Skytree Line local-station streets in the north where this collection lives. Used houses transact at about ¥101万/tsubo city-wide (floor-area basis, regression on 2,150 deals): Akayamacho ~123 and the Koshigaya station districts ~116 at the top, Gamo ~104; by age (Gamo series as the shape proxy), stock under 10 years runs ~139 and 30+-year stock ~65. Land by station: Shin-Koshigaya and Minami-Koshigaya ~¥80万/tsubo (their all-use average of ~99 and commercial points at 156–180 are not residential levels), Koshigaya-Laketown ¥65万, Koshigaya ¥60万, Gamo ¥55万, Kita-Koshigaya ~¥50万 — not the official catchment average of ~39, which is dragged by control-area and farmhouse-fringe points — Sengendai ¥42万 and Obukuro ¥40万. Land falls steeply with distance here (~−30% by a 10-minute walk, −45–50% by 16+), so the walk bands are Matsudo-like, not gentle.

Direction: rising gently and broadly — official residential prices +3.2% in the 2026 公示地価 survey (30 of 32 points up, none down), the fifth straight year of acceleration, with station-side points up 5–6% and the adjustment-area fringe flat. The defining hazard is water: eight flood-forecast river reaches cross this lowland city and the city publishes an inland-water inundation map as well — Laketown itself is flood infrastructure, built around a regulating reservoir — so both maps are the check on every card. One house in this collection sits in an urbanisation-control area and is scored and labelled as such. Honest negatives: no Koshigaya-specific liquefaction ranking exists and none is claimed, and the Ayasegawa fault's southern section was evaluated as not active, so no fault caution is printed. One record carries a feed anomaly (a stated 320m² building on a 100m² plot) and is marked unverified on its card. Sources: MLIT transaction records and official land prices via Utinokati and Totinokati/tochidai, Ekitan for train times.
