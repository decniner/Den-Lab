How Tadaima! works

Every city guide is researched by hand from the public portals (SUUMO, LIFULL HOME'S, atHome, goo住宅・不動産) on the date shown on its card, using the same brief and the same score everywhere:

PartPointsWhat it measures
Value45Price against real MLIT transaction benchmarks for that station and walk distance (land), or against the collection median (houses).
Location30Walk minutes to the nearest station, plus how well-connected that station is.
Space15Floor area for houses, plot size for land.
Confidence10Verified on the listing's own page vs an index page, and how recent the listing information is.

Major cautions — zoning traps, demolition costs, builder tie-ins — subtract points and are always printed on the card, never buried. No paid placement. We don't reuse listing photos; every card links to the original listing.

Roadmap これから

Tadaima! grows city by city, only when a city's research is done to the same standard — no empty pages, no fake coverage. Twenty-three cities are live. Why search and cards, not a map of Japan? Honest answer: with a handful of cities, a drill-down map is three taps to discover how much of Japan we haven't covered yet. The trigger set for revisiting it — around ten cities across several prefectures — has now been reached (23 cities, 7 prefectures), so a List/Map view is in design. The cards stay the default: they carry the counts and starting prices a map pin can't.
