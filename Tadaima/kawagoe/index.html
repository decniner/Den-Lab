How the ranking works

One score out of 100, built from four parts — the same maths for every property, with the reasons printed on each card. No paid placement, no hidden weighting. A major caution (for example land in an urbanisation-control area, or a house sold as a renovation project) subtracts 7 points and is always shown, never buried.

Kawagoe, in numbers

Kawagoe is Tokyo-facing: about 30 minutes to Ikebukuro on the Tobu Tojo Line, roughly 45–55 minutes to Shinjuku via Seibu's Koedo limited express or a direct JR Saikyo Line rapid. Three hubs anchor prices — Kawagoe, Hon-Kawagoe and Kawagoeshi stations — and prices fall off clearly with walk time: transacted land within 5 minutes of a station averaged ¥92.5万/tsubo in 2023–25, against ¥45.2万 at 11–15 minutes.

Used houses transacted at about ¥96–98万/tsubo in 2025–26 (average total ¥3,480万) after a soft 2025 (−4.7%), while land rose: official 2026 land prices were up +1% to +5% in every Kawagoe district, strongest near the centre and stations. New houses in the city typically ask ¥3,400–4,100万 for a standard 3–4LDK. Sources: MLIT transaction records via Utinokati, MLIT official land prices (基準地価, July 2026), goo listing averages, japan-guide.com for train times.
