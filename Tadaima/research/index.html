Research & methodology

Everything on this page is generated from the same data files the editions themselves load — the index below cannot drift from the dataset it describes. Prices are asking prices collected 2026-10-23 (see each edition for its research date and the corrections log for later fixes); benchmarks are MLIT transaction averages via Utinokati, 2023–2025.

このページは、各都市ページが読み込むのと同じデータファイルから自動生成されています。下の指数がデータセットと食い違うことはありません。現在、全572件のうち437件は物件ごとの詳細ページで確認済みです。スコアは価格の割安度（45点）・立地（30点）・広さ（15点）・情報の信頼度（10点）の合計100点で、重要な注意点1件につき7点を減点します。価格は売出価格、ベンチマークは国土交通省の取引事例（Utinokati経由、2023–2025年）です。訂正はすべて下部の訂正ログに公開しています。

Methodology

Each property's “worth a look” score is Value 45 + Location 30 + Space 15 + Confidence 10, minus 7 per major caution, rounded and clamped to 1–99. Value for land compares the asking ¥/坪 with a station benchmark (the station's MLIT transaction average blended with a walk-band figure); value for houses compares ¥/m² of floor area with the edition's own median for its type (new or used) — never with another city's. Location combines the station walk (a linear ramp) with a station tier of 2–8 points for Tokyo access and services. Confidence is 10/9/8 for detail-verified records by the age of the listing's information date (≤14 / ≤60 / >60 days at research time) and 6 for index-only records. A “major caution” is one that materially affects buildability, cost, legality or structural livability — a building condition, a no-rebuild status, an encroachment, a setback that eats the plot — and each one is printed on the card.

Verification standard: a record is “detail-verified” only when its facts were checked on the property's own listing page; the 2026-10-24 sweep additionally required at least three agreeing fingerprints (price, areas, station/walk, layout) before promoting an index record, and never promotes on a plausible-but-uncertain match. Portal rate limits are a stop rule, not an obstacle to route around.

A score, worked by hand

Shimohiroya 大字下広谷 — Used 5LDK (1982) — Sekisui light-steel build on a 205m² corner plot (Kawagoe h23, ¥1,480万, score 75)

  • Value 45.0 / 45 — ¥11.2万 per m² of floor vs ¥24.4万 median for house-used in this edition (ratio 0.46). The value curve is a straight-line interpolation between published knots (ratio 0.70 → 45, 1.00 → 24, 1.30 → 4 for houses; 0.45 → 45, 1.00 → 24, 1.60 → 4 for land).
  • Location 14.0 / 30 — walk 19 min to Tsurugashima station: 22 − (19 − 2) × 14/13, clamped to 8–22, plus the station tier 6 for Tsurugashima (tiers run 2–8 by the station's Tokyo access and services).
  • Space 15.0 / 15 — interpolated on floor area (132.0 m²) between the published knots.
  • Confidence 8 / 10 — source verified on its own detail page (information date 2026-04-14, researched 2026-11-02).
  • Major-caution penalty −7 — 1 major caution(s) × 7.
  • Total = 45.0 + 14.0 + 15.0 + 8 − 7 = 75 after rounding and clamping to 1–99.

Reproduce it: open the Kawagoe edition, sort by score, and the top card's “Why this score” panel shows the same components. The code is the shared app.js every edition loads — one implementation, no per-city variants.

The dataset, downloadable

Everything above is computed from a public dataset, and the dataset itself is public too. all-properties.json compiles every edition's records — the same file each city page loads, gathered into one — with each record's source portal, source URL, information date and verification status. Per-city files live at <city>/data/properties.json, and the scoring code that ranks them is the site's own shared/app.js (the score function). Facts belong to their listings; the compilation, scores and cautions are Tadaima!'s work — audit them, reuse them, and if you find an error, the corrections log below is where its fix will appear.
