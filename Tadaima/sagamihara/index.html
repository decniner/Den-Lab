How the ranking works

One score out of 100, built from four parts — the same maths for every property, with the reasons printed on each card. No paid placement, no hidden weighting. A major caution (for example land in an urbanisation-control area, or a house sold as a renovation project) subtracts 7 points and is always shown, never buried.

PartPointsWhat it measures
Value45Land: price per tsubo against MLIT transaction averages for that station and walk band (via Utinokati). Houses: price per m² of floor area against the median for new / used houses in this collection.

Freshness chips: a green New this week chip marks a property added at the latest weekly check; a Price ↓ chip marks a verified price cut since the previous check, with the amount. Both clear at the next weekly check — they describe the last seven days, not the property.

Sagamihara, in numbers

Sagamihara (a designated city; three wards — Midori, Chuo, Minami) prices as Tokyo's outer commuter ring with two genuine hubs. Used houses anchor at about ¥112万/tsubo city-wide (floor-area basis): Minami ward ≈¥125万 around Sagami-Ono, Chuo ward ≈¥111万, Midori ward ≈¥94万 on a catchment that mixes urban Hashimoto with the rural west — which is why this page scores land by station, not by ward. Land: Sagami-Ono ≈¥100万/tsubo (commercial-mixed at the front), Higashi-Rinkan ≈¥95万, Hashimoto ≈¥90万 — adjusted up from a raw ¥46万 print that is pure urbanisation-control and large-lot drag — then Sagamihara and Yabe at ¥80万, Fuchinobe and Kobuchi at ¥75万, stepping down the Sagamihara Line and the Ban'an corridor to Banda (¥42万) and the rural-fringe stations in the ¥30s. Age is the sharp adjuster for houses: under-10-year stock ≈¥137万/tsubo, 30+-year stock ≈¥73万, which is land value with a building on it.

Direction is up: official residential prices rose in all three wards in 2026 — Chuo +5.1%, Minami +4.5%, Midori +3.2% — and house transaction averages have climbed every year since the 2019 bottom. Two standing cautions. The city's east and south hold the usual plateau-edge package — sediment-disaster and flood checks apply lot by lot, and cards print them where listings disclose them. And the western fringe is urbanisation-control and rural land trading at ¥10万/tsubo: real prices for a different legal market, never blended into the corridor figures above. One data honesty note: two aggregator district prints above ¥200万/tsubo (Midori ward, Sagami-Ono) were composition artefacts and were rejected rather than averaged in. Sources: MLIT transaction records and official land prices via Utinokati, Ekitan for train times.
