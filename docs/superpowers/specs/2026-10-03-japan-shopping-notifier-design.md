# Japan shopping discount notifier

## Intent and authorization
Build and deploy a working daily Telegram digest of publicly purchasable physical products for Japan with at least 50% verified discount. The operator authorized routine implementation choices, use of existing GitHub access and Telegram secrets, one labeled test digest, commit/push, and schedule enablement. No purchases, account creation, secret disclosure, or changes to existing notifications.

## Architecture
Add an independent Python 3.12+ subsystem under `shopping-notifier/`, following the existing standard-library Python notifiers. Separate model/validation, network access, store verification, delivery/state, and CLI orchestration. Public Shopify catalogs discover candidates; each reported exact variant is revalidated with fresh product JSON and its public HTML product page. Start with Anker Japan, Keychron Japan, KINTO, UGREEN Japan, and Classical Elf; report individual failures and bounded coverage. Naturehike's Japan product feed redirects to HTML; Allbirds Japan migrated to another storefront. Neither is supported initially. Other stores need validated adapters, not generic unverified scraping.

## Verification and scope
Use Decimal JPY prices and compare the unrounded percentage against 50. Require positive exact prices, a variant-specific compare-at price supported by the product page, JPY structured offers matching the exact SKU/variant and sale price, current stock in both sources, and a live Japan shipping policy. Check expiry dates, quantity rules, physical shipping, tax-inclusive evidence, and product restrictions. Filter used/refurbished, memberships, coupons, subscriptions, trade-ins, financing, preorders, and digital products conservatively. Points never affect price. Manufacturer list-price and retailer comparison claims are labeled as such; neither proves usual-market savings. Unknown/ambiguous reference prices are rejected. Shipping is taken from freshly checked policies when unambiguous, otherwise explicitly unknown.

## Configuration and bounded coverage
JSON configuration controls enabled stores, categories/product types, excluded keywords, JPY price range, source page and product-verification caps, maximum ten digest entries, and meaningful-drop thresholds. Initially eight catalog pages of 250 products and twenty candidate product checks per store; limits are reported, not called exhaustive scans. One exact variant per product avoids ten sizes of the same garment. Category filtering uses store taxonomy with optional keyword matching. Include electronics, computer accessories, household goods, appliances, clothing, and portable power equipment; dedicated outdoor-store coverage is initially unsupported.

## Delivery and history
Reuse `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` and `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID` only inside Actions. Plain-text messages have safe UTF-16 length limits and product-boundary splitting. Display name, exact variant/retail unit, store/new retail condition, reference basis, tax-inclusive sale/reference, computed discount and yen savings, shipping, JST verification time, and direct variant URL. Rank by reference/verification evidence, then unrounded discount and savings. Suppress exact variants for seven days, except a drop of at least 5% AND JPY 100 (configurable). Explain deduplication when verified deals exist but are suppressed. No results with failed sources is explicitly incomplete; complete no-result scans say “No verified qualifying deals today”.

Keep history on dedicated `shopping-notifier-state` branch, separate from `main` and existing notifier state. Persist after each successfully delivered message, never before Telegram acknowledgement. Leave test-digest and dry-run history unchanged. Atomic local writes and an exclusive local file lock complement Actions concurrency. Fail visibly if history cannot be loaded/saved, rather than silently treating it as empty. Ambiguous Telegram POST failures are not blindly retried; explicit 429 responses may retry with bounded waits. Backups are uploaded on persistence failures.

## Scheduling and validation
New Actions workflow uses `0 22 * * *` (07:00 Asia/Tokyo); scheduled starts may be late. Support manual normal runs, dry runs, and labeled test digests. Use timeouts, bounded GET retries, per-host rate limits, robots rules, and independent source failures. No CAPTCHA bypass or paid services. Run offline tests, live dry run, existing notifier regression tests, one live Actions test digest, and confirm enabled workflow. Commit implementation to the default branch using existing authorized access.

## Final review refinements
Classical Elf's 定価 marker is treated as a retailer-displayed original/list comparison, not independently proven manufacturer MSRP. Add default 120-second per-source and 600-second overall scan budgets, with request timeouts and body reads bounded by the remaining deadline. Budget exhaustion is incomplete coverage, allowing delivery/history time within the 25-minute Actions limit. Category selection classifies product types/names rather than accepting every item from a mixed-category store.
