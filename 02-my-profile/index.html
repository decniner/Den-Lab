01Machine learning · Educational experiment

Tiny LLM from Scratch

A tiny language model, opened up step by step so its internal calculations can be seen.

Explore the experimentClose details＋

I use “The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog” as the training sentence. Keeping the model intentionally tiny makes it possible to follow the numbers instead of hiding them behind libraries.

In view

  • Tokenization
  • Embeddings
  • Hidden layers
  • Logits
  • Probabilities
  • Loss
  • Gradients
  • Backpropagation
  • Parameter updates

An educational experiment, not a production LLM.
